Delete your Backlot account

Account deletion must remove the associated cloud account and Creator data, not just sign you out or delete one backup.

Account deletion requests are not being accepted through this preview page.

The secure authenticated deletion service, verified support contact and request-receipt process are not live yet. Opening this page does not submit or queue a request. This is not an eligible Google Play account-deletion URL.

What the completed deletion flow must cover

  • Verify the requester controls the Backlot Creator account.
  • Stop further Creator sync writes and Voice Idea uploads to a deleting account.
  • Delete applicable Creator cloud snapshots, sync heads, account profile and app-owned Voice Idea objects through the supported Storage API.
  • Delete the related Backlot authentication account only after required cloud cleanup and verification.
  • Provide a truthful status/receipt and explain any lawful retention exceptions, if applicable.

What is separate

Your Google account and YouTube channel are independent and must not be deleted as part of Backlot account deletion. Personal Reminders stored on your phone, exported JSON backups, and original external media are separate local or user-managed files; a cloud-account request does not erase them automatically.

What you can do in the preview

Use Backlot's Data & Privacy screen to export Creator and Personal data separately, or sign out of your connected Creator cloud account. Signing out does not delete the cloud account or its stored data.

Before public launch, this page must gain a working user-accessible request path that functions even after the app is uninstalled, verified identity handling and an approved contact option.
