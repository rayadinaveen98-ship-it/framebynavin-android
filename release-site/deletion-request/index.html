What cloud account deletion means

When operational, a verified request will cover your Backlot cloud authentication account, Creator profile, stored Creator snapshots and app-owned Voice Idea recordings. A request receipt does not mean deletion is immediately complete.

This does not delete your Google account, YouTube channel, local Personal Reminders, separately exported backups or media stored outside Backlot. Export Creator and Personal backups separately before continuing.

1. Verify your account

Sign in with the same Google account connected to Backlot. Signing in alone does not delete anything.

2. Confirm permanent deletion

This irreversible operation requires a second confirmation. Your Google account is not deleted.

The confirmation controls become available only after verified sign-in and a live deletion-service status check.

3. Privacy and support

Read the draft privacy disclosure and the account-deletion status page. An approved developer contact and a public request service are required before launch.

Request permanent cloud-account deletion?

Backlot will request deletion of the currently verified account and associated Creator cloud data. Local Personal Reminders and separately exported files are not erased. This cannot be undone after processing.
