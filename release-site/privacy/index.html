Privacy at Backlot

Backlot is an Android creator workspace. Core projects and Personal Reminders work locally; some connected features send data to the providers you choose to use.

Draft — not an approved or published privacy policy.

These disclosures reflect current Android source inspection. A verified developer contact, third-party data/retention review and working account-deletion service are still required. Do not use this draft as the Play Store privacy URL.

1. Information stored on your phone

Backlot stores creator projects, scripts, ideas, planning details, saved frames, reminders and app settings locally. Personal Reminders and private notes are maintained separately from Creator cloud backups. Reminders may use local notifications, alarms, vibration and optional audio or speech features.

2. Optional Creator account and backups

When you choose to connect a Creator account, Backlot uses Google sign-in and Supabase authentication and cloud infrastructure. Creator account information and eligible creator data may be transmitted to Supabase, including projects/snapshots and app-owned Voice Idea recordings. Cloud syncing is not the same as an offline-only copy.

Signing out does not delete your account or cloud backups. A local backup does not itself delete cloud copies.

3. Connected services

  • YouTube: With authorization, Backlot can access selected channel statistics, performance insights and account information using Google/YouTube APIs. The available scopes and data accessed depend on the feature connected.
  • Google Calendar: With your permission, Backlot may read or create calendar entries for selected creator scheduling workflows. Calendar data may sync through your device's calendar provider.
  • Optional AI tools: AI features may transmit user-selected prompts and public video references to Google/Firebase AI services. Do not assume AI requests remain only on the device.
  • Spark voice: The configured on-device recognition path uses device voice recognition where available; microphone access requires permission. Availability and device speech-provider behavior may vary.

Before publication, validate actual SDK traffic, service data handling, optionality, retention, security and third-party disclosures. This is not a final Data Safety declaration.

4. Android permissions

The app requests permissions for appropriate features: notifications, recording audio, calendar access, exact alarms and reminder playback. Some reminders use full-screen notifications, foreground audio, vibration or boot recovery. Permission denial may limit those features. No permission is a promise of unrestricted background behavior on every device.

5. Exported backups and media

Creator and Personal Reminders have separate backup processes. Exported Personal Reminder JSON includes reminder titles and private notes and is not encrypted. Store it securely. Creator exports cover supported app-owned records, but external original project files and separately referenced media may require manual transfer. Exporting a file to another app or storage provider is a user-directed transfer.

6. Retention, deletion and user choices

Local items can be managed from within supported Backlot features. Cloud sign-out is not account deletion. Full Backlot account deletion is not yet available in the owner preview; its secure server-side deletion and external request process must be completed before public launch. See account deletion status.

Retention periods, any lawful exceptions, account-deletion response handling, production support contact and third-party service practices must be verified and approved before this becomes an operative privacy policy. No unsupported retention timeframe is claimed here.

7. Developer contact

The official developer support contact is pending owner confirmation. A private support email or personal identifier has not been invented or published. The final policy must identify an approved point of contact and explain how privacy requests are handled.
