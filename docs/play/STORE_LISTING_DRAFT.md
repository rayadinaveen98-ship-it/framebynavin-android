## Full description
Backlot brings your creative work together in one organized space.

Keep track of projects, capture ideas as they arrive, and plan what comes next. Build your own creative routine with reminders, a content workspace, and quick ways to return to your priorities.

What you can do:
- Organize creator projects, tasks and content plans.
- Capture ideas and manage your personal creative workflow.
- Set reminders with notification, voice or alarm options, subject to your phone's settings.
- Choose a gentle background track for voice reminders, including a local audio file.
- Create personal reminders separately from creator projects.
- View upcoming work and use optional home-screen widgets.
- Export Creator and Personal records through separate backup flows.

Optional connected features may allow access to supported Creator account backup, YouTube information or calendar workflows. Their availability depends on account permissions, service access and device capabilities.

Reminder delivery can be affected by Android notification, precise alarm and battery restrictions. Your device may not run every reminder at the exact requested time.

**About privacy:** Local Personal Reminders are separate from the Creator cloud workflow. Review the privacy policy and export backup files before changing devices.
